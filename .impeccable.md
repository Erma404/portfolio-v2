## Design Context

_Drafted from the brief given while building the site (2026-09); to be confirmed by Ernestine._

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and prospective clients (startups to large corporates) evaluating Ernestine Matjabo as a Product Owner / Product Manager / product builder. They skim on desktop between other candidates, sometimes on mobile from a LinkedIn link. Job to be done: understand in a minute what she ships, how she works, and how to contact her. Bilingual audience: English by default, French version at /fr.

### Brand Personality
Warm, precise, confident. The site should feel crafted rather than templated, with delight in small motion details (animated bento, typewriter footer, doodle canvas), but never at the expense of reading the work.

### Aesthetic Direction
Reference: the "Nuance 2.0" Framer portfolio (nuance-portfolio-v2.framer.website): cream background, serif display type, peach/orange gradients, dark cards and footer, floating pill navigation. Light theme. Existing palette: cream #fdf6ef, deep green accent #16412f, orange #e2632a, near-black #141414.

### Design Principles
1. Fidelity to the Nuance reference first; adapt, don't reinvent, unless asked.
2. Both languages are first-class: layouts must survive longer French labels.
3. Real content only: no invented figures or testimonials.
4. Motion supports reading and respects prefers-reduced-motion.
5. Mobile adapts rather than amputates.
